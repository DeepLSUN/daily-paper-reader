## 摘要
循环深度语言模型（如循环 Transformer）反复应用共享网络块以细化潜在表示，而无需生成显式的中间推理 token。然而，每一步都会在整个上下文上重新计算全注意力，重复进行代价高昂的全局路由。我们研究注意力路由如何随循环深度演变，并发现收敛时间尺度存在一致分离：注意力支撑集和分布显著早于隐藏状态和注意力输出趋于稳定。这表明循环推理分为两个阶段：早期发现稀疏工作集，随后在基本稳定的路由支撑集上进行表示细化。受此发现启发，我们提出 WISE（Working-set Inference with Support Exploitation，支撑集利用的工作集推理），一种免训练方法：在早期循环步骤中使用无限制注意力来发现块结构化工作集，然后在后续步骤中复用其支撑集，同时保持注意力权重和循环细化动态变化。受控干预表明，多步发现比首步选择产生更有效的工作集，并且支撑集复用比限制性更强的注意力复用形式更好地保持模型行为。在多跳问答基准上，WISE 基本保持了全注意力性能。匹配的上下文扩展实验表明，随着上下文变长、路由支撑集变得更稀疏，质量-效率权衡日益有利。在 4K 上下文下，稀疏注意力实现相比原生 FlashAttention 实现了最高 1.76 倍的后期步骤注意力加速。代码：https://github.com/tbn5pj/WISE_code。

## Abstract
Recurrent-depth language models, such as looped Transformers, repeatedly apply shared network blocks to refine latent representations without generating explicit intermediate reasoning tokens. However, each step recomputes full attention over the entire context, repeating costly global routing. We study how attention routing evolves across recurrent depth and find a consistent separation in convergence timescales: attention support and distributions stabilize substantially earlier than hidden states and attention outputs. This suggests two stages of recurrent inference: early discovery of a sparse working set, followed by representation refinement over largely stable routing support. Motivated by this finding, we introduce WISE (Working-set Inference with Support Exploitation), a training-free method that uses unrestricted attention during early recurrent steps to discover a block-structured working set, then reuses its support in later steps while keeping attention weights and recurrent refinement dynamic. Controlled interventions show that multi-step discovery yields more effective working sets than first-step selection, and that support reuse better preserves model behavior than more restrictive forms of attention reuse. Across multi-hop QA benchmarks, WISE largely preserves full-attention performance. Matched context-scaling experiments reveal an increasingly favorable quality-efficiency tradeoff as routing support becomes sparser with longer contexts. A sparse-attention implementation achieves up to a 1.76x late-step attention speedup over native FlashAttention at 4K context. Code: https://github.com/tbn5pj/WISE_code.

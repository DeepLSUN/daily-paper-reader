## 摘要
语音语言模型（SLMs）从预训练语言模型继承了强大的指令遵循能力，然而 ASR 专业化可能会显著损害这些能力。为了解决 ASR 与 QA 之间的这种权衡，我们提出了任务特定的同策略蒸馏（TS-OPD），该方法利用 ASR 专业化之前和之后的模型作为互补的 QA 和 ASR 教师。学生模型为 ASR 和 QA 分别生成任务条件化的轨迹，每条轨迹仅由对应的教师监督，从而减少两种监督信号之间的直接竞争。在基础 ASR、上下文 ASR 和 QA 上的实验表明，TS-OPD 在提升识别性能的同时保持了 QA 能力。此外，TS-OPD 在不同平衡系数下保持稳健，并能持续受益于蒸馏数据的增加。

## Abstract
Speech Language Models (SLMs) inherit strong instruction-following capabilities from pretrained language models, yet ASR specialization can substantially degrade them. To address this ASR--QA trade-off, we propose Task-Specific On-Policy Distillation (TS-OPD), which leverages models before and after ASR specialization as complementary QA and ASR teachers. The student generates separate task-conditioned trajectories for ASR and QA, each supervised only by its corresponding teacher, thereby reducing direct competition between the two supervision signals. Experiments on basic ASR, contextual ASR, and QA demonstrate that TS-OPD improves recognition while preserving QA capability. Moreover, TS-OPD remains robust across different balancing coefficients and continues to benefit from increased distillation data.

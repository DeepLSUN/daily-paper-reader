## 摘要
科学文献中包含了数十年的实验测量数据，但这些数据仍难以作为结构化数据供现代人工智能和数据驱动研究获取。这些信息大多分散在图片、图注、正文和表格中，需要先识别、关联并验证实验数据及其上下文，才能重新利用。在此，我们介绍 ERAF4XRD（Experiment Reader Agentic Framework for X-Ray Diffraction，X射线衍射实验阅读智能体框架），一个全自动的多模态（即图像和文本）多智能体框架，可从科学出版物中重建经验证的X射线衍射（XRD）记录。ERAF4XRD 下载并筛选文档，识别 XRD 图，提取元数据并将其链接到相应的实验数据，并使用独立的验证智能体依据源证据验证输出结果。在由273篇科学出版物组成、包含3,150个候选图的人工整理基准上，ERAF4XRD 对 XRD 图识别的准确率最高达到98.7%，并在22个字段中生成了1,400个元数据值。对最终验证记录进行的独立人工评估得到98.5%的精确率和90.7%的召回率，在443个受评估字段中未发现无证据支持的元数据。通过从信息抽取迈向对关联实验记录的重建与验证，ERAF4XRD 建立了一种自动化方法，可将已发表的科学信息转化为机器可读的实验数据集，用于人工智能和数据驱动科学。

## Abstract
The scientific literature contains decades of experimental measurements that remain difficult to access as structured data for modern AI and data-driven research. Much of this information is distributed across figures, captions, text, and tables, requiring experimental data and their context to be identified, connected, and verified before they can be reused. Here we introduce ERAF4XRD (Experiment Reader Agentic Framework for X-Ray Diffraction), a fully automated multimodal (i.e., image and text), multi-agent framework that reconstructs validated X-ray diffraction (XRD) records from scientific publications. ERAF4XRD downloads and screens documents, identifies XRD figures, extracts and links metadata to the corresponding experimental data, and validates outputs against source evidence using an independent validation agent. On a manually curated benchmark of 273 scientific publications containing 3,150 candidate figures, ERAF4XRD achieved up to 98.7% accuracy for XRD figure identification and generated 1,400 metadata values across 22 fields. Independent manual assessment of the final validated records yielded 98.5% precision and 90.7% recall, with no unsupported metadata observed among 443 evaluated fields. By moving beyond information extraction to the reconstruction and validation of linked experimental records, ERAF4XRD establishes an automated approach for transforming published scientific information into machine-readable experimental datasets for AI and data-driven science.
